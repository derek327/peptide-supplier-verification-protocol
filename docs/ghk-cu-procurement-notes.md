# GHK-Cu Research Procurement Notes

GHK-Cu (copper tripeptide) is a copper-bound tripeptide used widely in
research on skin and connective tissue models. Procurement notes below.

## 1. Molecule and form

| Item | Note |
|---|---|
| Structure | Copper(II) complex of the tripeptide glycyl-histidyl-lysine (GHK) |
| Form | Lyophilized powder, typically blue-to-purple/blue-green — the copper complex color is a quick visual check on arrival |
| Typical vial sizes | 10–100 mg research vials (larger than typical peptide vials because the peptide is short and cheap) |
| Copper content | ~1 copper ion per peptide; a properly complexed lot has consistent color |

## 2. Documentation checks specific to GHK-Cu

- **Copper complexation stated** — confirm the product is the GHK-Cu complex
  (not GHK peptide + free copper, which is a different product entirely).
- **Color/appearance specification** — the CoA should state the expected
  appearance; a lot that arrives colorless when blue was expected, or vice
  versa, deserves a hold-and-ask.
- **Purity and identity** — same baseline: HPLC method stated, identity
  confirmed. Copper-complex purity by HPLC can be supplemented by a copper
  content assay (ICP or colorimetric) — ask if your work is copper-dose
  sensitive.
- **Counterion and content** — net peptide/complex mass vs gross mass.

## 3. Practical arrival checks

1. **Color match** to the CoA appearance statement.
2. **Lot match** on vial vs CoA.
3. **Solubility quick note** — reconstitute a tiny aliquot only if your
   protocol requires; otherwise store lyophilized per stability statement.

## 4. Procurement red flags

- Supplier selling "GHK" and "GHK-Cu" interchangeably at the same price —
   they are different products for dosing purposes.
- CoA without appearance/color specification for a colored complex.
- Bulk pricing that ignores copper content variance across lots.

## 5. Checklist

- [ ] Complexation (GHK-Cu, not free GHK) stated
- [ ] Appearance/color spec on CoA
- [ ] HPLC purity with stated method
- [ ] Copper content assay available on request
- [ ] Vial lot matches CoA lot
- [ ] Storage statement (lyophilized, desiccated)
