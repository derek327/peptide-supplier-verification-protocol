# Supplier Audit Questionnaire — Research Peptide Procurement

Before placing a first order with a new peptide supplier, a structured audit
questionnaire is the fastest way to separate legitimate GMP-aligned
manufacturers from repackagers. This document is a ready-to-send
questionnaire organized by the areas that matter for research-grade
procurement. Each question is paired with the reason it is asked and the
answer a capable supplier should be able to give.

## 1. Facility & compliance

| # | Question | What the answer tells you |
|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Is synthesis performed at your own site or contracted? | Contracted synthesis is not disqualifying, but the responsible party must be named. |
| 1.2 | Do you hold a GMP certificate, and from which authority? | A named authority (e.g., national medicines agency) is verifiable; a self-issued "GMP policy" is not a certificate. |
| 1.3 | What is your ISO 9001:2015 scope? | Scope should include peptide manufacturing, not just trading. |
| 1.4 | Can you share your facility address and does it match your registration? | Repackagers often list a trading address with no production site. |

## 2. Synthesis capability

| # | Question | What the answer tells you |
|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | Which synthesis method do you use (SPPS, LPPS, recombinant)? | SPPS is standard for research peptides; the answer should be specific. |
| 2.2 | What is your typical batch size in grams? | Batch size should be consistent with the quantities you are buying. |
| 2.3 | Do you perform in-process purity checks during synthesis? | Coupling-efficiency monitoring is a hallmark of a real manufacturer. |
| 2.4 | What purification do you use (prep HPLC) and to what target purity? | Prep HPLC with a stated target (e.g., ≥ 98%) is the industry norm. |

## 3. Analytical / quality control

| # | Question | What the answer tells you |
|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | Which identity method do you use (LC-MS, MS/MS)? | Identity must be mass-confirmed per lot — "visual check" is not identity. |
| 3.2 | Is purity reported as HPLC area % at which wavelength? | 214 nm is the standard peptide backbone wavelength; 280 nm overstates purity. |
| 3.3 | Do you report peptide content (salt-corrected), not just chromatographic purity? | A 98% pure (area) peptide with acetate/TFA salt can be ~85% peptide by weight. |
| 3.4 | Which water-content method (Karl Fischer)? | Residual moisture matters for lyophilized stability claims. |
| 3.5 | Can you release an HPLC chromatogram with the CoA? | Raw chromatograms are hard to fake consistently — a real lab can share them. |

## 4. Batch & stability

| # | Question | What the answer tells you |
|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | How is lot numbering assigned? | Lots should map to a single synthesis run. |
| 4.2 | What stability data do you hold, and at which storage conditions? | Lyophilized peptides should show real −20 °C and 2–8 °C data, not a blanket "stable" claim. |
| 4.3 | How do you handle temperature excursions during storage? | There should be a written procedure, not improvisation. |
| 4.4 | What is your retest date policy? | Retest dates based on stability data are the useful signal. |

## 5. Documentation & traceability

| # | Question | What the answer tells you |
|---|---|---|
| 5.1 | What documents ship with each order? | Expect: CoA for the exact lot, MSDS, and a packing list with lot number. |
| 5.2 | Can you provide a certificate of conformity for raw materials? | Traceability to starting materials is a differentiator. |
| 5.3 | Do you retain reserve/retention samples? | Retention samples enable dispute resolution later. |
| 5.4 | Under what conditions would you accept an on-site or third-party audit? | A supplier that welcomes audits is auditable; one that deflects is not. |

## 6. Red flags — stop and verify further

1. CoA purity stated as a single number with no method, no wavelength, and no date.
2. "Identity: pass" without naming LC-MS or another mass-based method.
3. Lot number on the CoA does not match the label on the vial.
4. No facility address, or the address is a residential/commercial mailbox.
5. Answers that describe trading ("we source from vetted partners") rather than manufacturing.
6. Pressure to pay before documentation is provided.

## 7. Scoring quick reference

Score one point per fully documented, verifiable answer in sections 1–5.
A supplier scoring below ~12 of 20 should be re-audited or replaced before
any significant order. Keep the completed questionnaire with the order file —
it becomes part of the lot's audit trail.
