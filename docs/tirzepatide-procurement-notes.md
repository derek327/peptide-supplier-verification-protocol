# Tirzepatide Research Procurement Notes

Tirzepatide is a 39-amino-acid synthetic peptide with dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor
activity, widely supplied for metabolic research. Procurement notes below —
documentation and handling only.

## 1. Molecule and supply context

| Item | Note |
|---|---|
| Sequence | 39 amino acids, includes a C-terminal amide — confirm the supplier states the C-terminus form (acid vs amide) on the CoA |
| Form | Lyophilized powder, commonly acetate salt |
| Typical vial sizes | 1–15 mg research vials; bulk for cohort studies |
| Supply note | High research demand → grey-market volume high → documentation discipline matters more than for niche peptides |

## 2. Key documentation checks

- **C-terminal amidation stated** — biological activity is
  C-terminus-dependent; a CoA that does not state the terminal form is
  incomplete for this peptide.
- **LC-MS intact mass** — 39 residues with a stated amidation should give a
  predicted mass; the CoA mass should match within stated tolerance.
- **Purity ≥ 95% with method** — same baseline as other research peptides;
  method must be stated.
- **Peptide content %** — acetate counterion and residual water make the
  difference between gross and peptide mass; request it for molar dosing.

## 3. Aggregation / handling notes

Longer amphipathic peptides can aggregate under stress:

- Reconstitute gently; avoid vigorous vortexing.
- Aliquot immediately; avoid repeated freeze-thaw.
- Follow the supplier's storage statement (typically frozen, desiccated).
- If the lyophilized cake arrives collapsed or discolored, document and
  reject the lot — see `lyophilized-stability-notes.md`.

## 4. Compliance boundary reminder

Tirzepatide sits in a heavily regulated therapeutic space. For research
procurement:

- Order documentation on a research-grade basis ("for laboratory research
  use only").
- Do not request or accept documentation implying clinical use claims from a
  research supplier — that is a red flag about the supplier, not a benefit.

## 5. Checklist before ordering

- [ ] CoA states C-terminal amidation
- [ ] LC-MS intact mass within tolerance
- [ ] HPLC method stated (214–220 nm)
- [ ] Vial lot matches CoA lot
- [ ] Peptide content % available
- [ ] Research-use labeling understood
